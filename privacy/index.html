Privacy

Short and plain, like everything here.

What we keep

Only if you tick the box: your email address, and which guide you asked for. If you don't tick it, we keep nothing. You just see your guide.

Why

To email you the guide, then one short email a week about bedtime. Nothing else. We never sell or share your email.

Who stores it for us

Klaviyo, the service that sends our emails.

How long

Until you unsubscribe. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link at the bottom. Or reply to any of our emails and we'll delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, at any time, by replying to any of our emails. If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This site doesn't use tracking cookies. It loads its fonts from Google Fonts.
