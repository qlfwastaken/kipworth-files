Kid won't stay in bed? Here's the bedtime pass, and its one rule 🎟️

The pass is one card, given at lights out. It buys ONE trip out of his room: a cuddle, a drink, a fact about cheese. The rule: once it's spent, it's back to bed. If he comes out again, you walk him back. No chat.

Researchers tested it: kids given the card came out of their rooms less than kids without one (Moore et al., 2007, University of Nevada). The "nearly four trips a night, down to zero" in the video is one boy, from a smaller study (Freeman, 2006).
